        How the recipes were chosen

        Recipes came from Budget Bytes and Downshiftology, filtered for high ratings and short hands-on times. Ratings and review counts shown on each card were read off the source page. Seventeen of the thirty nights link to a specific published recipe; the other thirteen are written for this plan and are marked Built for this plan — they exist to use up what the sourced recipes leave in the fridge.

        On Serious Eats: you asked for it specifically, and I couldn't use it. Serious Eats blocks automated access, so neither search nor page fetches returned anything — I had no way to read a recipe or verify a rating there. Rather than cite pages I couldn't open, I sourced from two sites I could actually read. Two nights do use techniques Serious Eats popularised — the reverse sear on Aug 22 and hard-searing sliced steak in batches on Aug 19 — written from method, not copied from a page.

        Portions: recipes are listed at their published yields, mostly 4–6 servings. The plan assumes leftovers become lunch. Cooking for one or two? Halve the ground-meat nights and keep the sheet-pan ones whole — they reheat best.
